## 2. PILOT AGREEMENT OUTLINE (MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING)

A lightweight 3-page MOU governing the 30-day university pilot:
1. **Scope:** Provision of Lex Academy cloud/LAN software licenses for designated seminar groups.
2. **Commitments by Lex Academy:**
   - Free access during pilot period.
   - Comprehensive faculty onboarding and technical support.
   - Zero retention of unauthorized student personal data.
3. **Commitments by University Department:**
   - Provision of 1 instructor and minimum 10 participating students.
   - Completion of pre-test and post-test feedback forms.
   - Formal evaluation review by Department Chair at pilot conclusion.
