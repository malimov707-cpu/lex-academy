## 3. COST STRUCTURE ANALYSIS

### 3.1 Fixed Monthly Operating Costs (OPEX):
- **Core Cloud Infrastructure (Local UZ Cloud Server / VPS):** $150 – $300 / mo (~1.9M – 3.8M UZS).
- **Core Engineering & Platform Maintenance:** $1,800 – $2,500 / mo.
- **Legal Content Verification Honorariums (Per Scenario):** ~$80 – $150 per verified scenario.
- **Customer Success & University Support:** $400 – $800 / mo.

### 3.2 Variable Session Costs (COGS):
- **AI Token Utilization:** ~$0.015 per student trial session (using efficient OpenAI-compatible or local models).
- **Network Bandwidth & Storage:** Negligible (<$0.002 per session).
- **Gross Profit Margin:** **88% – 93%** on annual software licenses.
