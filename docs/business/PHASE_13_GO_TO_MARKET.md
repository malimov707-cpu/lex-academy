# PHASE 13 — GO-TO-MARKET (GTM) STRATEGY
**Institutional Sales Funnel, Marketing Mix & Commercial Execution Plan**
*Date: November 20, 2026 | Market: Uzbekistan*

---

## 1. INSTITUTIONAL B2B SALES CYCLE

The procurement cycle for Uzbek higher education institutions follows a 5-stage funnel:

```
[1. Department Chair Demo] -> [2. 30-Day Free Seminar Pilot] -> [3. Faculty Academic Council Endorsement] -> [4. Commercial Tender / Contract] -> [5. Campus Onboarding & Training]
```

### 1.1 Funnel Stage Details:
1. **Target Outreach:** Direct outreach to the Head of Department (*Kafedra mudiri*) for Civil Procedure or Criminal Procedure at TSUL and UWED.
2. **The "Zero-Risk Pilot":** Offer a fully facilitated 30-day trial with 1–2 seminar groups (no institutional financial commitment required).
3. **Evidence-Based Conversion Pitch:** Present the post-pilot report demonstrating $\ge 65\%$ instructor grading time saved and measured student advocacy gains.
4. **Contract Execution:** Leverage the university's academic and financial autonomy (*moliyaviy mustaqillik*) to finalize an annual SaaS contract under direct departmental procurement thresholds.
5. **Onboarding & Success:** Deliver on-campus training workshops for professors and teaching assistants.

## 2. MARKETING & COMMUNITY ACQUISITION MIX

1. **Student Ambassador Program (*Lex Academy Elchilari*):** Recruit 2 prominent student leaders from TSUL Moot Court Society and UWED International Law Club to demonstrate the platform at student orientation.
2. **Telegram Educational Channel (*Huquqiy Simulyatsiya*):** Publish weekly practical legal case dilemmas, highlighting statutory answers and linking to interactive scenarios on the platform.
3. **Annual National Virtual Moot Court Competition (*Respublika Virtual Sud Tanlovi*):** Sponsor a nationwide digital mock trial tournament hosted on the Lex Academy platform, judged by prominent advocates and retired judges.
