## 2. BREAK-EVEN ANALYSIS

- **Monthly Fixed Operating Burn (Year 1):** ~46,000,000 UZS (~$3,650 USD / month).
- **Contribution Margin per B2C Student:** ~80,000 UZS / month.
- **Break-Even Target:**
  $$\text{Break-Even} = 1 \text{ Full University License} + 120 \text{ Active B2C Subscriptions}$$
- **Milestone:** The business achieves cash-flow positivity upon securing its second institutional university partner.
