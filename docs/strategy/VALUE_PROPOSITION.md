## 1. VALUE PROPOSITION: LAW STUDENTS

| Core Pain Point | Current Inadequate Alternative | Lex Academy Value Proposition | Quantifiable Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zero courtroom/advocacy practice during degree** | Memorizing textbook articles, listening to passive lectures | Practice unlimited simulated civil, criminal, and economic trials on your own laptop with active adversarial roles. | **From 1 mock trial per university career to 20+ simulated trials per semester.** |
| **Fear of procedural errors & lack of confidence** | Learning by trial and error during stressful post-graduation jobs | Risk-free simulation environment where mistakes are highlighted privately by rubrics before entering a real court. | **70%+ increase in self-reported student confidence during trial advocacy.** |
| **No individual feedback on legal reasoning** | Receiving a single letter/number grade weeks after submitting an essay | Instant rubric breakdown analyzing fact identification, statutory relevance, objection validity, and argument clarity. | **Immediate post-session competency diagnostic with attempt-over-attempt comparison.** |
| **Unverified practical competence for employers** | Generic diploma transcript showing only theoretical exam scores | A verified digital portfolio and cryptographic certificate proving mastery across 20+ realistic legal scenarios. | **Differentiated CV portfolio demonstrating measurable practical competencies.** |
