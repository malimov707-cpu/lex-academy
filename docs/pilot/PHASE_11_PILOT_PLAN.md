## 2. PILOT PARTICIPANTS & COHORT STRUCTURE

### Cohort 1: Initial Clinical Seminar (Week 1–2)
- **Target Size:** 1 Law Professor / Instructor + 10 Law Students (3rd-year Civil Procedure or Economic Procedure seminar).
- **Format:** In-person university computer lab or local Wi-Fi classroom network.
- **Scenario:** *"Shartnoma majburiyati bo‘yicha o‘quv sudi"* (Civil contract dispute).

### Cohort 2: Expanded Faculty Cohort (Week 3–4)
- **Target Size:** 2 Instructors + 20–25 Students across 2 seminar groups.
- **Format:** Combination of in-class multi-user simulation and independent at-home solo AI case analysis.

---

## 3. SCHEDULED PILOT ACTIVITIES & PHASING

```
[Day 1: Onboarding & Pre-Test] -> [Day 2: Solo Practice / Case Prep] -> [Day 3: Live Classroom Moot Session] -> [Day 4: Rubric Review & Post-Test]
```

1. **Phase 1: Pre-Test & Confidence Baseline:** Students complete a 15-minute diagnostic assessing their understanding of contract breach burdens of proof and self-reported confidence in trial advocacy.
2. **Phase 2: Individual Exhibit Inspection:** Students review exhibits (`A-1`, `A-2`, `B-1`, `B-2`) and annotate key contractual clauses on their own devices.
3. **Phase 3: Synchronized Classroom Courtroom Trial:** The instructor leads a 60-minute trial session where the designated student judge, plaintiff advocates, defense advocates, and witnesses argue the dispute live.
4. **Phase 4: Post-Test & Assessment Review:** Students review their rubric score deltas; instructor evaluates performance reports; all participants complete standardized feedback forms.

---

## 4. MEASUREMENT METRICS & VALIDATION GATES

1. **Technical Completion Rate:** Target $\ge 90\%$ of participants complete the trial session without fatal client-side crashes.
2. **Time-to-Stage Transition:** Pacing efficiency under teacher control.
3. **Self-Reported Student Confidence:** Measured via 5-point Likert scale pre-test vs. post-test.
4. **Instructor Satisfaction:** Net Promoter Score (NPS) and willingness to use Lex Academy in official recurring seminars.
5. **System Usability Scale (SUS):** Target SUS score $\ge 75/100$.
