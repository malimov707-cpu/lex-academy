# PHASE 11 — STANDARDIZED PILOT FEEDBACK INSTRUMENT
**Survey Protocols for Law Students & Instructors Post-Simulation**
*Date: November 20, 2026*

---

## 1. STUDENT FEEDBACK PROTOCOL (POST-SIMULATION)

### Part A: Quantitative Usability & Confidence (1–5 Likert Scale)
1. *"I felt confident participating in the procedural trial stages."* (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree)
2. *"The evidence board and annotation tools made it easy to cite contract exhibits."*
3. *"The live turn feed made it easy to follow my classmates' arguments."*
4. *"The rubric evaluation gave me clear, actionable insights into my strengths and weaknesses."*
5. *"I would like to use this platform in my regular university seminar classes."*

### Part B: Qualitative In-Depth Inquiries
6. *"What was the single most difficult procedural moment during the simulation?"*
7. *"Did you encounter any technical delays, freezes, or confusing button states?"*
8. *"How did this simulation compare to traditional textbook seminar assignments?"*

---

## 2. INSTRUCTOR FEEDBACK PROTOCOL

### Part A: Teaching Efficiency & Control
1. *"How easily were you able to manage student role assignments and advance stages?"*
2. *"Did the platform reduce or increase your preparation and grading overhead?"*
3. *"Were the automated preliminary rubric scores aligned with your academic standards?"*

### Part B: Institutional Readiness
4. *"What administrative or technical barriers would prevent your department from adopting this tool across all seminar cohorts?"*
5. *"What additional legal areas (e.g., Criminal Procedure, Labor Law, Family Law) would you prioritize for future scenario development?"*
