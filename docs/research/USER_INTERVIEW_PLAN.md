# PHASE 1 — USER INTERVIEW RESEARCH PLAN
**Discovery Scripts, Non-Leading Question Protocols & Validation Metrics**
*Target Stakeholders: Law Students, Legal Instructors, University Administrators, Legal Employers*

---

## 1. RESEARCH METHODOLOGY & INTERVIEW GUIDELINES

To eliminate confirmation bias and prevent fabricated validation, user interviews must adhere to the **Mom Test** principles:
- **Never ask leading questions** (e.g., *"Would you like an AI courtroom simulator?"*).
- **Focus on past concrete behavior** rather than speculative future intent (e.g., *"Walk me through the last practical exercise you completed..."*).
- **Measure friction and time expenditure** in existing workflows.
- Record exact quotes, friction anecdotes, and actual workarounds currently employed.

---

## 2. INTERVIEW PROTOCOL: LAW STUDENTS (SEGMENT A)

### Screening Criteria:
- Currently enrolled 2nd, 3rd, or 4th-year law student (or Master's / LL.M student) in Uzbekistan.

### Core Question Script:

#### A. Past Experience & Behavioral Reality
1. *"Walk me through the last practical case study or mock trial you participated in at university. How was it organized?"*
2. *"How many times during the last academic semester did you have the opportunity to actively speak or argue a case in front of a class or instructor?"*
3. *"When you are given a case assignment, what is the exact step-by-step process you follow to solve it?"*
4. *"Where do you find your legal references and laws when working on an assignment?"*

#### B. Friction & Difficulties
5. *"Think of a time when you received feedback on a legal drafting or practical exercise. What was helpful, and what was missing?"*
6. *"When you imagine walking into a real district court or a client meeting right after graduation, what specific procedural step or task makes you feel least prepared?"*
7. *"What prevents you from practicing more court cases or legal drafting right now?"*

#### C. Tooling & Willingness to Pay
8. *"What digital tools, websites, or apps do you currently use for your legal studies on a daily basis?"*
9. *"Have you ever spent your own money on any legal study guides, bar prep materials, online courses, or tools? If so, what was it and how much did it cost?"*
10. *"If a software tool simulated legal cases and gave you detailed feedback on your arguments, what would make you use it every week? Conversely, what would make you abandon it after one try?"*

## 3. INTERVIEW PROTOCOL: LAW INSTRUCTORS & PROFESSORS (SEGMENT B)

### Screening Criteria:
- Teaches procedural law (Civil Procedure, Criminal Procedure, Economic Procedure) or substantive law (Contract Law, Criminal Law, Labor Law) at an Uzbek higher education institution.

### Core Question Script:

#### A. Current Seminar & Practical Workflows
1. *"How do you currently run practical seminar sessions with groups of 25–35 students?"*
2. *"When organizing a mock trial or practical case, how do you assign roles and ensure every student actively participates?"*
3. *"How much time do you spend preparing a single practical case scenario for your students?"*

#### B. Assessment & Grading Bottlenecks
4. *"How do you evaluate student performance during practical exercises? What rubrics or criteria do you use?"*
5. *"How long does it take you to grade practical assignments for a typical seminar group, and how detailed is the feedback you are able to give each individual student?"*
6. *"What is the hardest part about scaling practical exercises across multiple student cohorts?"*

#### C. Institutional Adoption & Constraints
7. *"If you were introduced to a digital simulation platform that manages role assignments and generates preliminary grading rubrics, what would be your biggest technical or pedagogical concern?"*
8. *"What administrative approvals or curriculum alignment requirements would you need before using such a tool in your official classes?"*

## 4. INTERVIEW PROTOCOL: LEGAL EMPLOYERS & SENIOR PRACTITIONERS (SEGMENT E)

### Screening Criteria:
- Managing partner, senior advocate, or in-house legal director in Uzbekistan who has hired or supervised junior lawyers/interns within the past 18 months.

### Core Question Script:

#### A. Competency Gaps in Law Graduates
1. *"When you hire fresh law graduates from Uzbek universities, what are the most critical practical tasks they struggle to execute independently during their first 90 days?"*
2. *"Can you recall a specific instance where an intern or junior associate made a fundamental practical or procedural error? What caused it?"*
3. *"How do graduates perform when asked to review a messy contract or cross-examine a witness compared to citing legal articles from memory?"*

#### B. Professional Development & Training Investment
4. *"How does your firm or company currently train new legal hires? How many partner/senior hours are spent on onboarding?"*
5. *"What would make a candidate stand out as 'practice-ready' during an interview or case study test?"*
6. *"Would your firm value a verified digital transcript or competency portfolio demonstrating a graduate's performance across 20+ realistic trial and contract simulations?"*
