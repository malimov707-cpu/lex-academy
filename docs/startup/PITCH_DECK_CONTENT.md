### Slide 1: Title & Vision
- **Header:** Lex Academy (*Huquq AI*)
- **Subheader:** The Practical Legal Education & Simulation Platform for Central Asia.
- **Presenter:** Startup Execution Team.

### Slide 2: The Critical Problem
- **The Bottleneck:** Legal education in Uzbekistan is 90% theoretical code memorization.
- **The Physical Limit:** TSUL has over 6,000 students and only 2 physical mock courtrooms. Students participate in only 1 mock trial in 4 years.
- **The Market Pain:** Employers (law firms, banks, courts) spend 6–12 months retraining graduates who lack basic procedural and evidentiary skills.

### Slide 3: The Solution — Lex Academy
- An interactive digital simulation platform where law students practice, simulate, evaluate, and master real-world legal competencies.
- **Three Integrated Modes:**
  1. **Court Mode:** Interactive 3D/2D procedural courtroom with state machine and dynamic exhibit board.
  2. **Class Mode:** Instructor-orchestrated multi-user trials supporting 10 distinct student roles.
  3. **AI Mode:** Socratic legal reasoning tutor and guided case analysis.

### Slide 4: Live Product Demonstration
- Walkthrough of *"Shartnoma majburiyati bo‘yicha o‘quv sudi"*:
  - Student selects exhibit `A-1` (Contract), highlights late-delivery clause, enters objection.
  - Teacher facilitator advances procedural stage from `EVIDENCE` to `CLOSING`.
  - Live feed updates instantly across all student screens.
  - Rubric engine outputs attempt-over-attempt competency delta.

### Slide 5: Product Defensibility & Moat
- **Jurisdictional Accuracy:** Conforms directly to the Civil, Economic, and Criminal Procedure Codes of the Republic of Uzbekistan.
- **Offline & Low-Bandwidth Resilient:** Runs over standard local Wi-Fi without cloud dependencies.
- **Local Legal Corpus:** 51 pre-indexed statutory texts and local textbooks prevent AI hallucinations.

### Slide 6: Market Opportunity (Uzbekistan & Central Asia)
- **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** $45M (Legal education and vocational training across Central Asia).
- **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM):** $12M (25,000+ law students, 12 universities, 4,500 licensed advocates in Uzbekistan).
- **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM):** $1.8M (Top 5 law universities + 3,000 active student subscribers by Year 3).
