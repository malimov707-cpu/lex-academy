### Prohibition on Inadvertent Common Law Import
The platform strictly prohibits importing foreign Common Law concepts (e.g., *voir dire*, *Miranda warnings*, *summary judgments under Federal Rule 56*, *punitive damages*) into Uzbek proceedings unless a scenario is explicitly labeled as a comparative international law moot trial.

## 2. OFFICIAL LEGAL REPOSITORIES & INTERNAL LOCAL LIBRARY

### 2.1 Authoritative Source: LexUZ
All statutory references link to and derive from **LexUZ (lex.uz)**, the official national legislative database maintained by the Ministry of Justice of Uzbekistan.

### 2.2 Built-in Local Library (`/library`)
The platform embeds **51 curated legal resources**, with **38 full statutory texts locally readable** directly within the web application:
- Constitution of Uzbekistan.
- 18 core codes (Civil, Criminal, Economic Procedure, Civil Procedure, Criminal Procedure, Labor, Tax, Customs, Family, Housing, Land, etc.).
- 11 major laws (Advocacy, Courts, Arbitration, Mediation, Personal Data, etc.).
- 2 peer-reviewed open textbooks (Intellectual Property & Business Law under CC BY 4.0 licenses).

## 3. CONTENT GOVERNANCE & EDITORIAL BOARD

1. **Content Creators:** Scenarios and case briefs are drafted by qualified legal educators, docents, or practicing advocates (*advokatlar*).
2. **Double-Blind Legal Verification:** Before publication, a scenario must be reviewed by an authorized instructor (`verified_by`) who confirms statutory validity on LexUZ.
3. **Statutory Deprecation Watch:** When Uzbek legislation is amended or updated (e.g., the adoption of the new Labor Code), the scenario version is tagged for review; existing completed sessions preserve their immutable historical statutory snapshot.
